Free Stuff You Can Actually Take Home in Dhaka

Physical things — saplings, books, medicine, food, furniture, electronics, samples — that are given away at no cost in Dhaka. Researched September 2026.

Two different categories below. Some giveaways are open to anyone (saplings, product samples, secondhand giveaways, mosque iftar). Others are means-tested or targeted at low-income people, students, or patients — taking from those when you don't need them takes from someone who does. Each section says which is which.

1. Free tree saplings — open to anyone

The most reliably free physical item in Dhaka. You can usually walk away with several plants.

  • Bonayan (BAT Bangladesh's programme, running since 1980): has given away 130+ million fruit, timber and medicinal saplings nationwide, free of charge, and planned 5 million more in 2026. It distributes via ward councillors, schools, NGOs and community events — e.g. 5,000 saplings handed to a single Mirpur ward office at once. Track their distribution events and contact the local ward office.
  • Forest Department (বন অধিদপ্তর), Dhaka Division: runs nurseries and joint plantation drives; free saplings are commonly distributed at National Tree Plantation Week / Briksharopon Karmasuchi (usually around June–July, monsoon planting season).
  • Dhaka North & South City Corporations: DNCC is running a 500,000-tree drive and DSCC a 300,000-tree, five-year plan — both distribute saplings to residents at ward-level events.
  • Green Savers Association: organises plantation drives around Dhaka (e.g. 1,600 trees at Diabari with DNCC, IDCOL and the Forest Department) and gives away plants at campaigns.
  • National Tree Fair (বৃক্ষমেলা), Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, typically June–July: most stalls sell, but government and sponsor stalls hand out free saplings, especially on inauguration and closing days.

How to catch them: follow your ward councillor's office, DNCC/DSCC Facebook pages, and Bonayan announcements; distributions are advertised only days in advance.

3. Free books and printed material

  • NCTB school textbooks — every student from pre-primary through SSC gets a full set of brand-new textbooks free on 1 January each year, including Braille editions and Chakma, Marma, Tripura, Garo and Sadri language versions. (Targeted: enrolled students.)
  • Ekushey Book Fair (Bangla Academy + Suhrawardy Udyan, February): entry is free and publishers, embassies, NGOs and government stalls hand out free catalogues, literary magazines, posters and promotional booklets; some stalls give away free copies on the closing days.
  • Cultural centres — Alliance Française (Dhanmondi/Gulshan), Goethe-Institut (Dhanmondi), EMK Center (Gulshan) and the Indian and Iranian cultural centres regularly hand out free magazines, exhibition catalogues and posters at events.
  • Bidyanondo Foundation runs free libraries and its own printing press, distributing free educational materials to underprivileged students. (Targeted.)

4. Free medicine, vaccines and health items

EPI vaccines — free for everyone

All Expanded Programme on Immunization vaccines are supplied free by the Government of Bangladesh and administered free at government facilities and participating hospitals. Example: Dhaka Community Hospital (190/1 Boro Moghbazar) gives the full government EPI schedule to newborns and children 0–15 months free of charge, 9 am–1 pm, Saturday to Tuesday. You take home the vaccination card too.

  • Government hospital outpatient departments (DMCH, Suhrawardy, Mugda, Kurmitola etc.) dispense a list of essential medicines free to patients, subject to stock.
  • Community clinics & urban primary healthcare centres give free contraceptives, ORS, iron/folic acid and deworming tablets.
  • Free blood-group testing at Sandhani (Sandhani Bhaban, 33/2 Nilkhet, Babupura) — you leave with your blood-group card. Sandhani and the Quantum Voluntary Blood Donation Program (31/V Shantinagar) supply voluntarily-donated blood to patients rather than selling it.
  • Bidyanondo's "One Taka Treatment" provides consultation and medicine for ৳1. (Targeted.)
  • Free medical camps (eye, diabetes, dental) with free medicine and free reading glasses are run regularly by Lions Clubs, Rotary, Sandhani and hospital foundations — watch local notices.

5. Free food you can carry away

  • Baitul Mukarram National Mosque, Motijheel: free iftar for ~2,000 people daily through Ramadan (chickpeas, puffed rice, potato chop, beguni, peyaju, jilapi, plus a 250 ml water bottle each). Open to anyone who shows up.
  • High Court Mazar Mosque: free iftar for roughly 1,500–2,500 people daily in Ramadan, and two free meals a day to about 1,500 people year-round.
  • Bidyanondo's Ek Takay Ahar (One Taka Meal), hub at House 13, Road 2/B, Pallabi R/A, Mirpur: a full meal packet for ৳1, deliberately priced so recipients keep their dignity. Serves street children, elderly and destitute people across 8 districts. (Targeted — donate rather than take.)
  • Shrines and large mosques distribute tabaruk/shinni after Friday prayers and on urs days — free and take-away.
  • Qurbani meat at Eid-ul-Adha: distribution to neighbours and the needy is religiously mandated, and large volumes are handed out across every neighbourhood.

6. Free samples and promotional giveaways

Brand activations in Dhaka hand out real product, not just leaflets — and they happen weekly:

  • Supermarket sampling: Meena Bazar, Shwapno, Agora and Unimart host in-store sampling booths — e.g. CowBell's free milk sampling at Meena Bazar.
  • Street/park activations: City Group's Tutti Twist campaign gave out 1,500 branded balloons plus goodie bags at Gulshan 2, Rabindra Sarobar (Dhanmondi), Shadhinata Chattar (300 Feet), Versity Chattar (Diabari) and Aftabnagar.
  • Door-to-door / neighbourhood sampling: Radhuni ran a free sampling campaign for its Ready Mix Khichuri Masala across multiple Dhaka locations in 2025.
  • Trade fairs: Dhaka International Trade Fair (Purbachal, January–February) and food/agro expos are the single densest source of free samples, sachets and branded merchandise — entry is ticketed but cheap.
  • University campuses (DU, BUET, NSU, BRAC): telecom and FMCG brands routinely run stalls giving away SIMs, data bundles, snacks and merchandise during club events and job fairs.

8. Practical playbook

  1. Saplings are the easy win — free, plentiful, and nobody's need is displaced. Start with your ward councillor's office and the June–July plantation season.
  2. Set up alerts: Facebook Marketplace saved search at ৳0 for your area, plus membership in Desperately Seeking Dhaka and local area groups (Uttara/Mirpur/Dhanmondi "buy sell exchange" groups).
  3. Time it around events: Ekushey Book Fair (Feb), Trade Fair (Jan–Feb), Tree Fair (Jun–Jul), Ramadan (iftar), Eid-ul-Adha (meat).
  4. Turn up early — nearly every giveaway here is first-come-first-served and unadvertised beyond a day or two's notice.
Caution: "free" listings and giveaway posts are a common vector for advance-fee and courier scams in Bangladesh — never pay a "delivery charge" or "booking fee" for a free item, and collect in person in a public place.
