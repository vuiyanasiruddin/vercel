Free in Dhaka

Free things to do, see, learn and use around Dhaka — plus the cheapest ways to move between them. Researched September 2026.

Scope note: "free to get things around Dhaka" is ambiguous, so this covers both readings — free things to get/do in Dhaka and getting around Dhaka cheaply. Prices and opening hours in Bangladesh change often; verify before you go.

2. Museums — when they're free (and what they cost otherwise)

  • Bangladesh National Museum (Shahbagh): free every Tuesday for students from pre-school through class 10 plus accompanying teachers, roughly 10:30 am–4:00 pm, under the "Children's Day" scheme. Otherwise ৳40 adult / ৳20 child (Bangladeshi), ৳300 SAARC, ৳500 other foreigners; children under 3 and visitors with disabilities enter free. Closed Thursday.
  • Grounds and plazas of the National Museum, Shilpakala Academy and the Liberation War Museum are free even when the galleries are ticketed — useful if you only want the sculpture, murals and public spaces.

3. Free culture: galleries, concerts, talks

Bengal Shilpalay / Bengal Foundation free

House 42, Road 27, Dhanmondi. Rotating exhibitions in the Quamrul Hassan Exhibition Hall, typically open daily 4–8 pm, free entry. Also runs classical music and literature programmes.

Bangladesh Shilpakala Academy mostly free

Segunbagicha. National Art Gallery exhibitions, folk-art shows, theatre and music in the Experimental Theatre Hall — most exhibitions are open to the public at no charge; some theatre productions sell tickets.

Alliance Française de Dhaka & Goethe-Institut Bangladesh free

Both in Dhanmondi (AF also has a Gulshan branch). Concerts (Fête de la Musique, Jazz Day), film screenings, gallery shows — routinely advertised "free entry, open to all", sometimes first-come-first-served or with email registration. Check their event pages weekly.

Amar Ekushey Book Fair free

Bangla Academy grounds + Suhrawardy Udyan, all of February (extended into March in 2026). Entry has always been free; readings, author sessions and the crowd itself are the attraction.

Pohela Boishakh & other street festivals free

Bengali New Year (14 April) Mangal Shobhajatra parade from the Charukala campus, plus Ramna Batamul dawn concert — all free, all public.

4. Free guided walks

  • Dhaka Greeters (part of the International Greeter Association): volunteer locals give a personalised 2–4 hour walk for up to 6 people, genuinely free and explicitly no tipping. Request via internationalgreeter.org.
  • GuruWalk lists "free walking tours" in Dhaka — note these are tip-based, not truly free.

6. Free/near-free essentials

  • Food: Bidyanondo Foundation's Ek Takay Ahar ("One Taka Meal") serves meals for ৳1 to street children, elderly and destitute people, with a hub in Mirpur (Pallabi). They also run One Taka Bazaar and One Taka Treatment.
  • Blood: Sandhani (Sandhani Bhaban, Nilkhet, Babupura) and the Quantum Voluntary Blood Donation Program (Shantinagar) supply voluntarily-donated blood and offer free blood-group testing — the main alternative to unsafe paid donors.
  • Prayer + rest: mosques city-wide offer free wudu, washrooms and shade; several large ones distribute free iftar in Ramadan.

8. A free day in Dhaka — sample route

  1. 6:30 am — Ramna Park dawn walk (free)
  2. 8:30 am — Dhaka University campus: Curzon Hall, Arts quadrangle, TSC tea (tea ~৳10)
  3. 11:00 am — Old Dhaka on foot: Shankhari Bazar, Armenian Church courtyard, Ahsan Manzil facade (free)
  4. 4:00 pm — Sadarghat at the golden hour; cross-river boat for a few taka
  5. 6:00 pm — Free exhibition at Bengal Shilpalay or an Alliance Française concert, Dhanmondi
  6. 8:00 pm — Hatirjheel walkway and fountains (free)

Caveats

  • Museum fees, park access and event calendars change frequently; the National Museum's fees were last doubled by notification, and Tuesday free-entry is a recent scheme.
  • "Free walking tours" on commercial platforms are tip-expected; only the Greeters programme is explicitly no-tip.
  • Some public spaces have been renamed or restricted amid political changes (e.g. Chandrima Udyan / Zia Udyan) — check current status locally.
  • Metro pass discount figures differ between sources (10% vs 30%); confirm at a station counter.
